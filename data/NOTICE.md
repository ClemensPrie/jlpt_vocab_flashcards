# Vocabulary data attribution

This derived dataset is shared under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0). See `source/openjlpt/LICENSE` for the license text.

It contains entries from [OpenJLPT](https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT), which incorporates JMdict and KANJIDIC2 (EDRDG), Jonathan Waller's JLPT Resources at [tanos.co.uk/jlpt](https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/), and example sentences from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org). OpenJLPT's full source notice is kept in `source/openjlpt/NOTICE.md`.

It also includes vocabulary entries drawn from the workspace's N1–N5 CSV files, N3–N5 topic JSON lists, and the vocabulary lists in the five Tanos PDF resources. The merged records include the contributing file, topic, or PDF page in their `sources` field. Original examples retain their Tatoeba attribution where available; examples written for entries without a usable source pair are marked as generated in the data.
